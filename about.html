Verification Desk

Garment Quality Control Standards

Phase 01 // Thread Tension Testing

We test individual cotton threads in stress clamps to verify tensile strengths, preventing early seam bursts and stitch snaps.

Phase 02 // Warp Drop Calibration

We hang fabric rolls on gravity frames to calculate warp flow speeds, ensuring linen blends drop in neat folds.

Phase 03 // Hydrophobic Mist Sprays

Fabric layers are tested with water mist to verify DWR roll-off coatings, protecting delicate wool fibers from damp weather.

Phase 04 // Alignment Ratio Grids

Jacket patterns are scaled against laser layout grids, verifying sleeve biases to maintain symmetrical shoulder curves.
